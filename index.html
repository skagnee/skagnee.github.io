Things I've built

US Traffic Accident Analysis

Python, Pandas, Scikit-learn, Seaborn, Matplotlib

  • Explored a 386K-row sample of a 7.7M-record dataset across 49 states to find accident trends and high-risk regions.
  • Cleaned the data with imputation, VIF-based multicollinearity reduction, PCA, and hybrid outlier detection.
  • Used K-Means, DBSCAN, and GMM, and found a dominant accident-condition cluster covering 85% of records.
  • Turned severity, weather, and time and location patterns into transportation-safety insights.

Obesity Risk Classification

Python, Scikit-learn, XGBoost

  • Built a multiclass pipeline on 2,100+ records to predict obesity risk.
  • Used DBSCAN and Isolation Forest for outliers, and PCA, t-SNE, and LDA for dimensionality reduction.
  • Tuned 6 models with GridSearchCV. XGBoost reached 97.8% accuracy and a 0.977 macro F1, ahead of Random Forest and SVM.
